WhatsApp Reminders & Alerts
How it works: For every ACTIVE loan the next unpaid installment's due date is calculated (loan start date + installment number). If the due date is tomorrow an Advance Reminder (vel_fin_advance_reminder) is sent. If the due date has passed a Delay Alert (vel_fin_delay_alert) with the exact overdue days is sent. Use Preview first to see exactly who will receive a message; Send asks for confirmation and never sends the same reminder twice on the same day.
